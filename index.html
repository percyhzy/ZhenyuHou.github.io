Zhenyu Hou

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at George Mason University (GMU), working in the RobotiXX Lab under the supervision of Prof. Xuesu Xiao. My Ph.D. is co-supervised with Polytechnique Montréal (affiliated with Université de Montréal), with Prof. Giovanni Beltrame (Mila) as my joint supervisor.

Before joining GMU, I was a research assistant at the FASTLAB of Zhejiang University from December 2023 to August 2026, supervised by Prof. Fei Gao and Prof. Chao Xu, and also worked in collaboration with Differential Robotics. I received my Bachelor's degree in Cyber Security from the Civil Aviation University of China in June 2024, mentored by Zhijun Wu.

zhou2@gmu.edu · zhenyu.hou@etud.polymtl.ca

Research Interests

My research interests lie in field robotics, autonomous navigation, robot learning, and motion planning. I aim to enable ground, legged, and aerial robots to move safely and efficiently through uneven, unstructured, and vertically challenging terrain by combining traversability perception and trajectory optimization with learning-based methods.

At FASTLAB, my work spanned real-time traversability assessment for off-road ground robots, learning-based trajectory generation on uneven terrain, collision metrics for trajectory optimization, trajectory planning for tractor–trailer vehicles, and perception and motion planning for aerial robots and large-scale swarms.

My current research directions include:

  • Off-road autonomous navigation on uneven and vertically challenging terrain
  • Learning-based trajectory generation with differentiable trajectory optimization
  • Reinforcement learning for legged and wheeled-legged robots
  • Cross-embodiment navigation and cooperative aerial–ground navigation
